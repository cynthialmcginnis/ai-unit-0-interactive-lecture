Unit 0: An Interactive Journey Into Artificial Intelligence

You Already Use AI Every Day

📍🚗

Open a navigation app. Type a destination. A route appears in seconds.

It accounts for traffic, road closures, and the way you drive.

That’s AI, running several kinds at once.

What’s Happening Under the Hood?

  • Collecting speed reports from thousands of phones on the road
  • Building a live traffic picture in real time
  • Running an algorithm to compare possible routes
  • Predicting the fastest route based on patterns
  • Learning your habits to suggest your usual route

You already know how this app behaves. You’ve watched it reroute you around a crash, and you’ve seen it get the arrival time wrong.

Understanding the Hierarchy

Artificial Intelligence (AI)
↓
Machine Learning (ML)
↓
Deep Learning

Every deep learning system is ML. Every ML system is AI. But not all AI is ML.

Three Terms You’ll Use Constantly

⚙️
Algorithm
The set of steps the system follows to learn or decide.
📊
Training Data
The examples the system learns from.
🧠
Model
The learned pattern the system uses on new cases.
Example: Spam Filter
  • Training Data: Thousands of emails marked spam or not spam
  • Algorithm: Finds patterns like certain phrases, suspicious links, or unknown senders
  • Model: Scores a new email and decides where it goes

How AI Reasons

📋
Symbolic AI
Follows rules written by people. Easy to check, but breaks when a case isn’t covered.
🎓
Machine Learning
Learns patterns from data instead of following a fixed rule list.

Three Types of Machine Learning

✅
Supervised
Learns from labeled examples such as spam/not spam.
🔍
Unsupervised
Finds patterns in unlabeled data without a prewritten group label.
🎮
Reinforcement
Learns from rewards and penalties after trial and error.

AI Through the Decades

1950
The Turing Test
Alan Turing proposes a test for machine conversation.
1956
Birth of AI
The term “artificial intelligence” is coined at Dartmouth.
1974–80
First AI Winter
Hype outpaces capability, and funding dries up.

AI Through the Decades

1980s
Expert Systems Boom
Rule-based systems capture business knowledge and become profitable.
1987–93
Second AI Winter
Rules prove hard to maintain, update, and scale.
1997
Deep Blue
IBM’s chess system beats Garry Kasparov.

The Modern AI Era

2012
Deep Learning Breakthrough
AlexNet wins the image recognition challenge by a wide margin.
2016
AlphaGo
DeepMind’s system defeats Lee Sedol in Go.
2017
Transformer
The model design behind today’s large language models.
2022
ChatGPT
Generative AI reaches the public in a major way.

What Changed?

❌

Early AI

Write intelligence down as rules.

✅

Modern AI

Learn from data and improve with examples.

Why now?

It worked once computers got faster and data got much bigger.

Narrow AI vs General AI

🎯

Narrow AI

  • Built for one task or a small set of related tasks
  • Excellent inside its boundary
  • Useless outside that boundary
❓

General AI

  • Would learn all kinds of tasks
  • Would act like a human across many fields
  • Does not exist yet

The GenAI Plot Twist

ChatGPT can write emails, explain taxes, and debug code. It looks broad—but it still fails in ways a person would not.

GenAI still fails in real ways
  • Invents sources that do not exist
  • Forgets simple facts during a conversation
  • Cannot reliably check its own work against reality

Breadth is not the same as general intelligence.

Key Takeaways

  • You use AI every day in navigation, recommendations, email, and more.
  • AI is a broad field: symbolic rules, machine learning, and deep learning.
  • Modern AI learns from data instead of just following written rules.
  • History shows cycles of hype, setbacks, and new breakthroughs.
  • Today’s AI is narrow: excellent at one task, limited beyond it.
  • GenAI looks broad, but it still fails in ways humans would not.
