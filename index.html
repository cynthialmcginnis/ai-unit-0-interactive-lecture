You Already Use AI Every Day

📍🚗

Open a navigation app. Type a destination. A route appears in seconds.

It accounts for traffic, road closures, and the way you drive.

That's AI, running several kinds at once.

What's Happening Under the Hood?

  • Collecting speed reports from thousands of phones on the road
  • Building a live traffic picture in real time
  • Running an algorithm to compare possible routes
  • Predicting the fastest route based on patterns
  • Learning your habits to suggest your usual route

You've watched it reroute you around a crash. You've seen it get the arrival time wrong. This unit gives you the vocabulary to explain what's happening.

Understanding the Hierarchy

Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Any system that does work we associate with human thinking
↓
Machine Learning (ML)
Systems that learn patterns from data
↓
Deep Learning
Neural networks with many layers

Every deep learning system is ML. Every ML system is AI. But not all AI is ML.

Three Terms You'll Use Constantly

⚙️
Algorithm
The set of steps the system follows to learn or make decisions
📊
Training Data
The examples the system learns from
🧠
Model
The learned pattern the system uses on new cases
Example: How a Spam Filter Works
  • Training Data: Thousands of emails marked spam or not spam
  • Algorithm: Finds patterns like phrases, links, unknown senders
  • Model: Scores new emails and decides where they go

How AI Reasons: Three Approaches

📋
Symbolic AI
Follows rules written by people. Easy to check, but breaks when cases don't match the rules.
🎓
Machine Learning
Learns rules from data. Can handle new situations the rules didn't anticipate.

Three Types of Machine Learning

✅
Supervised
Learns from labeled examples (spam/not spam)
🔍
Unsupervised
Finds patterns in unlabeled data on its own
🎮
Reinforcement
Learns through trial and error with rewards

What AI Does: Four Categories

💬
Natural Language Processing

👁️
Computer Vision
Face unlock, medical imaging, object detection
🤖
Robotics
Warehouse robots, surgical assistants, drones
⭐
Recommendation Systems
Netflix suggestions, shopping, social media

AI Through the Decades

1950
The Turing Test
Can a machine hold a conversation you can't tell apart from a person's?
1956
Birth of AI
The term "artificial intelligence" is coined at Dartmouth
60s-70s
Early Wins & Promises
Checkers programs learn, ELIZA imitates therapists. Hype builds.
1974-80
❄️ First AI Winter
Promises unmet, funding cuts. Programs can't handle language or common sense.

AI Through the Decades (Continued)

1980s
Expert Systems Boom
Rule-based systems capture specialist knowledge. AI becomes a business.
1987-93
❄️ Second AI Winter
Expert systems are expensive, hard to update, and can't learn. Hand-written rules hit a ceiling.
1997
Deep Blue Defeats Kasparov
IBM's AI beats the world chess champion. A breakthrough in narrow AI.

The Modern AI Era

2012
Deep Learning Revolution
AlexNet wins image recognition by a wide margin. GPUs + data = power.
2016
AlphaGo Beats Lee Sedol
DeepMind's AI masters Go—a game experts thought would take 10 more years.
2017
The Transformer
Google researchers publish a foundation for today's large language models.
2022
ChatGPT Goes Public
GenAI reaches millions in weeks. AI becomes mainstream.

What Changed?

❌

Early AI

Write intelligence down as rules

Breaks when cases don't match rules

✅

Modern AI

Learn from data

Adapts to new patterns

Why Now?

This shift only worked once computers got fast enough and data got big enough.

Narrow AI vs General AI

🎯

Narrow AI

Every AI today

  • ✓ Built for specific tasks
  • ✓ Excellent at one thing
  • ✗ Useless outside that boundary
  • ✓ Face unlock, navigation, spam filters
❓

General AI

Doesn't exist yet

  • → Would learn across any field
  • → Could handle any intellectual task
  • → Would think like humans do
  • → Researchers disagree if/when it will happen

The GenAI Plot Twist

ChatGPT can write emails, explain taxes, debug code...

Looks general, right? Not quite.

GenAI Still Fails in Human Ways
  • Invents sources that don't exist
  • Forgets simple facts within a conversation
  • Can't verify its work against reality
  • Doesn't know when it's wrong

Breadth ≠ General Intelligence

AI Technologies Today

Three drivers: Faster hardware, Better algorithms, More data

💬
LLMs
Engines of GenAI chatbots. Learn from text, generate word by word.
👁️
Computer Vision
Reads medical scans, inspects parts, guides robots and vehicles.
🎨
GenAI
Creates new content. Images from text, music, code from descriptions.
🚗
Autonomous Systems
Sense, decide, act. Self-driving cars, delivery robots, drones.
🎮
Reinforcement Learning
Learns by trying, getting rewards and penalties. RLHF makes chatbots better.
⚙️
NLP
Handles tone, context, meaning. Translation, assistants, moderation.

Key Takeaways

  • You use AI every day—navigation, face unlock, spam filters, recommendations
  • AI is a broad field: symbolic rules, machine learning, and deep learning
  • Modern AI learns from data, not hand-written rules
  • History shows cycles: hype → failure → new approach → breakthrough
  • All today's AI is narrow—excellent at one task, useless outside it
  • GenAI looks general but still fails in ways humans wouldn't
